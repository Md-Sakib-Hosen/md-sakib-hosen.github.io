            ASUS RT-AX52 OpenWrt 25.12.6 (`apk`): HFO vs. SQM CAKE, Quad DoH & 480K+ AdBlock-Fast

            CASE STUDY 03 · SMARTPHONE-FIRST LUCI OPERATIONS & LAN-ISOLATED TELEMETRY
          

          

            Mobile-First Router Administration & Telemetry: Yet Another LuCI App, OppsError404 & Prometheus
          

          

            Built for zero-friction smartphone administration of OpenWrt 25.12.6 using Yet Another LuCI App over `rpcd`/`uhttpd`, paired with Jerrykuku Argon Theme (v2.4.3) and the OppsError404 Visual Suite (`luci-app-dashboard`, `luci-app-client-monitor`, `luci-app-easy-setup`). Long-term observability combines vnStat2, nlbwmon, collectd, and a LAN-isolated Prometheus Lua Exporter (`:9100`) alongside hardened LAN-only `ttyd` (`192.168.1.1:7681`).
